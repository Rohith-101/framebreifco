# Quick Reference: Prompts for Any Brand (Cheat Sheet)

**Use this when you need to quickly generate prompts for a brand without the full workflow.**

---

## The 3-Concept Framework (All Brands)

Every brand uses the same 3 strategic angles:

| Concept | Focus | Use Case | Typical Emotion |
|---------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **1. ORIGIN/HERITAGE** | Where it comes from, materials, craftsmanship | Brand storytelling, education, trust-building | "I understand the quality" |
| **2. LIFESTYLE/ASPIRATION** | How it's used, who uses it, feeling it gives | Instagram, conversions, desire | "I want this" |
| **3. FUNCTION/PROOF** | What it does, evidence it works, specs | E-commerce detail, proof | "This actually works" |

---

## Super Quick Brand Input (2 Minutes)

Answer these 5 questions and you're ready:

1. **What's your product?**  
   *(e.g., "Organic cotton t-shirt" or "Vitamin D gummy")*

2. **Who's your audience?**  
   *(e.g., "Urban women 25-35, values sustainability")*

3. **What are 3 words for your brand?**  
   *(e.g., "Timeless, Authentic, Conscious")*

4. **What are your brand colors?**  
   *(e.g., "Off-white, charcoal, sage green")*

5. **Who are your 3 main competitors?**  
   *(e.g., "Nicobar, Mango, CottonWorld")*

**That's it.** Feed this into the tool or use it to create concepts manually.

## Prompt Formula (DIY - No Tool Needed)

If the tool isn't working, use this formula to write prompts manually:

### Concept 1 Prompt Template (Heritage/Origin)

```
[Product type] flat lay/close-up showing [hero material/ingredient]. 
Include [specific elements that show origin/quality]. 
Shot in [lighting style] showing [texture/detail]. 
Colors: [your brand colors]. 
Style: [brand aesthetic - luxury, minimal, authentic, etc.].
Avoid: [what you don't want].
```

**Example - Nykaa Serum:**
```
Luxury skincare product flat lay featuring a Nykaa Vitamin C Serum bottle 
surrounded by hero ingredients: fresh turmeric root (cut to show golden interior), 
vibrant green tea leaves, and a backlit lemon slice. Shot in warm golden hour 
studio lighting on marble surface. Colors: warm gold, cream, deep emerald green. 
Style: high-end beauty photography. Avoid: clinical vibes, plastic packaging, 
harsh shadows.
```

### Concept 2 Prompt Template (Lifestyle/Aspiration)

```
Portrait/lifestyle shot of [ideal customer] actively using/wearing [product] 
in [typical setting]. Feeling: [emotion]. Lighting: [natural/golden/minimal]. 
Colors: [your palette]. Style: [relatable/premium/authentic]. 
Avoid: [overly staged/fake/generic].
```

**Example - UNBOUND T-Shirt:**
```
Portrait of a woman in her 30s, sitting by a minimalist bedroom window in soft 
morning light, wearing an oversized cream-colored organic cotton t-shirt. 
She looks calm, confident. Feeling: timeless, quality. Lighting: natural window 
light. Colors: off-white, charcoal accents, sage green. Style: authentic, 
relatable luxury. Avoid: overly styled, runway aesthetic, heavy makeup.
```

### Concept 3 Prompt Template (Function/Proof)

```
Macro/close-up photograph of [product detail that proves function]. 
Show [evidence of quality/efficacy]. Lighting: [controlled/dramatic/soft]. 
Colors: [brand palette]. Style: [transparent/scientific/luxury]. 
Focus on: [what makes your product unique].
```

**Example - VITD Gummies:**
```
Extreme macro close-up of a plant-based Vitamin D gummy, glossy and translucent. 
Include ingredient list visible in background (blurred). Show the gummy's 
crystal clarity and purity. Lighting: studio backlit to catch shine. Colors: 
warm amber, cream. Style: scientific yet luxury (cosmetics transparency photography). 
Focus on: clean ingredients, no artificial anything.
```

## Copy-Paste Prompts by Category

### Skincare Product

**Concept 1 - Ingredient Story:**
```
Luxury flat lay of a premium serum bottle surrounded by its natural ingredients: 
fresh botanicals, pure oils, and raw materials. Warm golden studio lighting. 
Marble surface. Hero ingredient should be visible and textured. Colors: warm 
gold, white, green accents. Style: high-end beauty packaging photography. 
Shallow depth of field.
```

**Concept 2 - Glowing Skin:**
```
Close-up portrait of a woman with visibly radiant, dewy skin in soft natural 
morning light. Expression: calm, confident. Skin shows healthy glow (not artificial). 
Serum bottle visible but blurred in background. Colors: warm skin tones, soft 
gold light. No heavy makeup visible. Style: editorial beauty photography. 
Lighting: soft window light.
```

**Concept 3 - Proof/Clarity:**
```
Extreme macro Photography of clear, luminous serum droplet on glowing skin. 
Droplet is sharp, backlit to show translucent glow. Skin shows natural fine 
texture (real, not poreless). Focus: serum droplet only. Colors: golden, 
translucent. Style: luxury cosmetics transparency shot. Ultra-shallow depth of field.
```

### Fashion Product

**Concept 1 - Material/Craftsmanship:**
```
Close-up macro shot of fabric texture showing weave, thread detail, quality 
of material. Natural lighting to highlight fiber. Could include: label, tag, 
hand holding fabric edge. Colors: natural fabric tones. Style: artisanal, 
handmade aesthetic. Depth: shallow, focus on texture.
```

**Concept 2 - Wearing/Lifestyle:**
```
Person wearing the clothing item in everyday setting (not runway). Setting: 
could be commute, coffee shop, home, work. Natural, candid moment. Lighting: 
natural, warm. Person's expression: normal, confident, real. Focus: how garment 
moves/fits on body. Style: lifestyle photography, relatable.
```

**Concept 3 - Product Detail/Styling:**
```
Flat lay of the clothing item styled with complementary lifestyle objects 
(shoes with socks, jacket with scarf, shirt with accessories). Arrangement 
tells a style story. Lighting: even, natural or studio. Colors: cohesive, 
brand-aligned. Style: aspirational but accessible.
```

### Food/Supplement Product

**Concept 1 - Source/Origin:**
```
Farm or garden scene showing ingredient in natural state. Could be: vegetables 
in soil, herbs fresh-picked, grains in field, mushrooms on farm. Close-up detail 
of raw ingredient. Lighting: natural daylight, golden hour. Colors: natural, 
earthy. Style: authentic, showing "where it comes from" story.
```

**Concept 2 - Ritual/Meal Moment:**
```
Food/supplement in a real social moment: breakfast table with coffee, desk 
lunch break, kitchen counter morning routine. Person eating/consuming if relevant. 
Natural, unstaged feel. Lighting: natural, warm. Colors: food-appropriate, 
appetizing. Style: real-life, relatable, not over-styled.
```

**Concept 3 - Product/Nutrition Clarity:**
```
Close-up of product (gummy, powder, jar) with ingredient list or nutrition 
label visible. Product details clear: texture, color, freshness. Could show: 
powder texture, gummy shine, bottle label. Lighting: clear, professional. 
Colors: true to product. Style: transparency, clarity, "you know what you're getting."
```

### Tech Product

**Concept 1 - Technology/Innovation:**
```
Close-up of tech detail: circuit board, sensor, interface, button, or mechanical 
element. Shows the engineering inside. Lighting: studio, dramatic. Colors: 
tech-neutral (grays, blacks, silvers). Style: innovation aesthetic, precision. 
Shallow depth of field, focus on the tech element.
```

**Concept 2 - In-Use/Lifestyle:**
```
Person actively using the tech product in realistic context: working at desk, 
traveling, creating content, exercising. Lighting: natural, realistic. 
Product integration feels seamless, not forced. Expression: focused, enjoying. 
Colors: everyday realistic. Style: lifestyle, "this is how I use it daily."
```

**Concept 3 - Design/Materials:**
```
Product detail shot showcasing design quality: precision engineering, premium 
materials, sustainability elements. Macro photography of stitching, seams, 
material weave, logo. Lighting: studio, even. Colors: brand-aligned. 
Style: precision, craftsmanship, "built to last."
```

## Quick Debug: If Output Doesn't Match

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| "Too generic, doesn't feel like my brand" | Add specific brand personality words to prompt (e.g., "minimalist," "playful," "premium") |
| "Colors are wrong" | Replace "nice colors" with exact color names (e.g., "warm gold #D4A574, cream #F5F1E8") |
| "Looks too staged/artificial" | Add "natural," "candid," "authentic," remove "perfect," "magazine," "overly styled" |
| "Doesn't show what I want" | Rewrite with more specificity (e.g., instead of "modern," say "minimalist Scandinavian aesthetic") |
| "Too bright/dark" | Add lighting type: "golden hour," "soft window light," "studio lighting," "overcast daylight" |
| "Looks like a stock photo" | Add "authentic," "real," "not stock photo," remove generic elements |
