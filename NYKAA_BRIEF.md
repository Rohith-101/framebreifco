# Brand Brief Template — NYKAA TEST RUN

## PRODUCT & BRAND DETAILS

**Brand Name:** Nykaa (Nykaa Beauty)  
**Product Name:** Plant Power Vitamin C Serum

---

## PRODUCT

**Core Features/USP:**
- 15% stabilized Vitamin C + turmeric + green tea extract
- Plant-based, clean beauty (no parabens, sulfates, silicones)
- Fast-absorbing, brightening results in 2 weeks
- Dermatologically tested for sensitive skin

**What problem does it solve?**
- Dull, uneven skin tone from pollution, sun exposure, stress
- Women want brightening without harsh chemicals or irritation

**Price Positioning:**  
Mid-to-premium (₹1,200–1,500 / ~$15 USD)—higher than mass market, lower than international luxury

---

## TARGET AUDIENCE

**Primary Demographic:**
- Female, 25–35 years old
- Urban India (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad)
- Working professionals, digitally native, Instagram-active
- Household income: ₹10L–30L+/year

**What do they value most?**
- Natural + effective (no greenwashing; proven results)
- Transparent ingredient lists
- Indian/South Asian authenticity (not copying Western beauty standards)
- Sustainability (but willing to pay for quality)

**Where do they discover products?**
- Instagram (micro-influencers, beauty creators)
- Amazon/Nykaa app (e-commerce browse)
- Word-of-mouth (beauty forums, Reddit)
- YouTube tutorials (skincare routines)

---

## BRAND PERSONALITY & VISUAL IDENTITY

**3 Words Describing Brand Tone:**
- Authentic
- Aspirational (but relatable)
- Scientific (without being clinical)

**Brand Values:**
- Indian heritage + modern science
- Clean beauty without compromise
- Inclusivity (works for all skin tones)
- Transparency (ingredient focus, not hype)

**Color Palette:**
- Gold/warm bronze (premium, natural, warmth)
- Cream/off-white (clean, minimalist, luxury)
- Deep green (nature, freshness, growth)
- Accent: Turmeric yellow (heritage, uniqueness)

---

## MARKET CONTEXT

**Main Competitors (5):**
1. **Plum** — Direct competitor, wellness-focused Indian beauty, "no nonsense" tone
2. **Derma Co** — Clinical, high-performance, ingredient-focused
3. **Re'equil** — Science-backed Indian indie brand, minimalist
4. **WOW Skin Science** — Playful, trendy, multi-category (viral on Instagram)
5. **Fixderma** — Dermatological, clinical, anti-aging focus

**Your Unique Angle vs. Them:**
- **Vs. Plum:** Nykaa is premium beauty ecosystem (not just personal care)—Vitamin C is hero product, not side offering
- **Vs. Derma Co:** Nykaa leans into *lifestyle* + *heritage* more; Derma Co is pure clinical
- **Vs. Re'equil:** Nykaa has brand heritage + retail presence; Re'equil is indie (more "cult" positioning)
- **Vs. WOW:** Nykaa is premium + serious; WOW is trendy + casual
- **Vs. Fixderma:** Nykaa targets lifestyle/beauty; Fixderma targets problem-solving (acne, sensitivity)

**What You WON'T Communicate:**
- Clinical/medical (not a dermatological treatment brand)
- Luxury exclusivity ("only for rich people")—accessible aspiration
- Fake feminism or greenwashing trends
- Overly sensual or sexualized beauty imagery

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

**What Feeling Should This Image Evoke?**
- "This is what healthy, glowing skin looks like—and I can achieve it"
- Modern but rooted (not sterile, not overly aesthetic/fake)
- Confidence without arrogance
- Fresh start, quick results

**Where Will This Image Be Used?**
1. **E-commerce tile** (Amazon, Nykaa.com product page)
2. **Instagram feed/carousel** (campaign launch, new product intro)
3. **Website hero section** (landing page, above-the-fold)
4. **Paid ads** (Instagram/Facebook paid boosts)

**Expected KPI:**
- CTR % (click-through rate on e-commerce)
- Engagement rate on Instagram (likes, saves, shares)
- Conversion rate (e-commerce landing page test)

---

## NOTES FOR AI & DESIGN TEAM

**Tone References:**
- Look to: Nykaa's existing Instagram feed, current ad campaigns
- Avoid: Generic beauty stock photos, overly glamorous celebrity beauty, clinical lab aesthetics

**Visual Direction:**
- Authenticity > perfection (slight texture, real skin, natural lighting preferred)
- Inclusivity mandate: ensure skin tone representation across shots

**Production Notes:**
- Product must be visible and hero'd (not buried)
- If featuring skin/face: clearly shot of serum beneficiary (glowing skin = proof)
- Ingredient callouts OK (turmeric gold, green tea—these are differentiators)
