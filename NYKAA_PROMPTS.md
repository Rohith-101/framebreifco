## PROMPT 1: "GOLDEN POTENTIAL" (Ingredient Story)

### Main Prompt (for Midjourney / DALL-E 3)

```
Flat lay product photography of a premium golden serum bottle surrounded by its 
natural hero ingredients. The composition centers a minimalist glass dropper bottle 
with cream and gold label (reading "Nykaa Vitamin C Serum"), positioned on warm 
marble surface. Around the bottle: a fresh turmeric root cut in half showing bright 
golden interior, vibrant green tea leaves, a backlit lemon slice showing translucency, 
and a small glass vessel with drops of clear golden serum. Warm golden hour 
backlit studio lighting creating soft shadows and warm glow on marble. Color palette: 
warm gold, cream, deep emerald green, turmeric yellow. Shallow depth of field with 
bottle and turmeric sharp, other ingredients slightly soft. Natural, luxurious, 
high-end beauty photography style. No people, no text visible on label, clean studio 
setting, professional beauty packaging photography.
```

### Negative Prompt

```
Avoid: Clinical or sterile look, bright whites, cool fluorescent lighting, plastic 
packaging, excessive blur, stock photo aesthetic, artificial or synthetic-looking 
ingredients, busy or cluttered composition, watermarks, blurry labels, harsh shadows, 
overly saturated colors, fake or wilted ingredients, cartoon style, 3D render, 
filters or Instagram effects
```

### Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (landscape, ideal for website hero or social media feed)
- **Best For:** Website hero section, Instagram feed carousel lead image, Pinterest pin
- **Camera Style:** High-end beauty product photography, studio setup, similar to Glossier or Nykaa app aesthetic
- **Color Correction Needed:** Ensure turmeric's golden tones pop without losing natural feel
- **Typical Usage Size:** 1920x1080px (web) or 1080x1080px (Instagram square)

## PROMPT 3: "CLARITY UP CLOSE" (Science / Proof)

### Main Prompt (for Midjourney / DALL-E 3)

```
Extreme macro close-up photography of luminous, hydrated, translucent skin (cheek or 
forehead) with a single crystal-clear droplet of golden serum perched on the surface, 
backlit and refracting light to show glossy, luxurious finish. The serum droplet is 
the focal point—sharp, glistening, with visible light refraction. Skin shows natural 
fine texture (pores visible but skin is smooth, clear, and radiant), no visible 
blemishes or imperfections, even skin tone. Just the edge of a glass or metal serum 
bottle applicator visible at top frame, very blurred (context only). Professional 
macro photography with ultra-shallow depth of field, controlled studio lighting 
sidelit or backlit to catch droplet shine. Color palette: warm bronzy skin tones, 
translucent golds, hint of soft green undertones, cream accents. Premium beauty 
ingredient transparency photography. High magnification lens, buttery smooth bokeh,
```

### Negative Prompt

```
Avoid: Over-retouched or poreless skin (should look real, not artificial), dull or 
matte serum droplet, cloudy or opaque serum, visible makeup or foundation, harsh 
lighting creating unflattering shadows, clinical or medicinal aesthetic, visible 
blemishes or skin imperfections (unless authenticity is goal), visible fingerprints 
or dust, blurry droplet, cartoon or illustrated style, 3D render, extreme 
saturation, filters or Instagram effects, visible text or watermarks, dull or 
yellowed tones, cool-toned lighting, busy background, multiple droplets, wide-angle 
distortion effects
```

### Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:3 or 1:1 (vertical square preferred for mobile/Instagram Stories)
- **Best For:** Product detail page (zoomed section), Instagram Stories/Reels, Pinterest, testimonial carousel, "proof" section of web page
- **Technical:** Macro lens, shallow depth of field (aperture f/2.8 or shallower), controlled studio lighting key light
- **Skin Tone:** Warm/medium skin tone, South Asian or warm undertone representation to match primary audience
- **Typical Usage Size:** 1080x1080px (Instagram), or vertical 1080x1350px (Stories/Reels)

## TESTING & ITERATION GUIDE

### How to Test These Prompts:

**Step 1: Run in Your Image Generator**
- Test each prompt individually in Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Adobe Firefly
- Generate 3–4 variations per prompt (use seed variation or just re-run)
- Aim for 2 strong outputs per concept

**Step 3: Brand Fit Review**
Ask Nykaa stakeholders:
- "Does this feel like Nykaa?" (brand personality match)
- "Would this sell the product?" (conversion potential)
- "Is it authentically beautiful or overly retouched?" (tone check)

**Step 4: If Output Misses, Adjust Prompt**

**Common Issues & Fixes:**

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Droplet looks dull/not glossy | Add "sparkle," "glisten," "light refraction," or adjust studio lighting description |
| Woman's skin too filtered | Remove "retouched," add "natural texture visible," "fine pores visible" |
| Colors too cool/clinical | Change "studio light" to "golden hour light," specify 4000K warmth |
| Too many ingredients (Concept 1) | Reduce props list: "Only turmeric, green tea, lemon" |
| Background too busy | Specify "soft blur," "out of focus," "minimal background" |
